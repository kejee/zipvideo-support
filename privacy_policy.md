## Introduction

ZipVideo ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Data Processing

ZipVideo is designed to prioritize user privacy:
- **Local Processing**: All video compression is performed locally on your device.
- **No Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or video files to any external servers.

## Permissions

To function correctly, the app may request the following permissions:
- **Photo Library**: To select videos for compression and save compressed results.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
