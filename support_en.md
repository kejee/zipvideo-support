# ZipVideo Support

## App Description

ZipVideo is a professional video compression tool that uses H.265 (HEVC) encoding technology to significantly reduce video file size while maintaining quality.

### Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🎬 **H.265 Encoding** | Advanced video compression technology for smaller file sizes |
| 📱 **Local Processing** | All processing happens on your device - no uploads, no servers |
| ⚡ **Real-time Preview** | Preview videos before and after compression |
| 🔧 **Parameter Control** | Adjust bitrate, resolution, frame rate, and more |
| 💾 **Auto Save** | Compressed videos automatically saved to your photo album |

---

## How to Use

### Step 1: Select Video
Tap "Select Video" button and choose a video from your photo album.

### Step 2: Adjust Parameters
Configure compression settings:
- **Bitrate**: Target video bitrate (100-50000 kbps)
- **Encoder**: H.264 or H.265
- **Resolution**: 720P, 1080P, or Original
- **Frame Rate**: 15-60 fps

### Step 3: Start Compression
Tap "Start Compression" and wait for the process to complete.

### Step 4: View Result
Compressed video is automatically saved to "ZipVideo" album in your photo gallery.

## FAQ

### Q: Why is compression slow?
A: H.265 encoding provides better compression but requires more processing time. You can choose H.264 for faster compression.

### Q: Video quality dropped significantly?
A: Try increasing the target bitrate or selecting a higher quality encoder setting.

### Q: Compression failed?
A: Please check if the video file is corrupted, or try adjusting compression parameters.

### Q: Does the app upload my videos?
A: **No!** All video processing is done locally on your device. Your videos never leave your device.
